# Staff ML Platform Engineer — Anthropic

## About the role
We're hiring a Staff ML Platform Engineer to lead the design of our internal feature
platform and model serving infrastructure. You will work cross-functionally with
research scientists, MLEs, and product engineering to ship high-availability ML
systems that serve millions of users.

## Responsibilities
- Architect and own the end-to-end feature store and online inference stack.
- Drive design and implementation of multi-tenant model serving on Kubernetes.
- Partner with research to productionize new model architectures.
- Establish SLOs, observability, and incident response practices for ML services.
- Mentor and grow a team of senior engineers; influence platform roadmap.
- Collaborate with security and compliance on data handling and PII boundaries.

## Required qualifications
- 7+ years of software engineering, including 3+ years building production ML
  systems.
- Deep experience with one of: Kubernetes / KServe / Triton / Ray Serve.
- Strong systems programming background in Python and one of Go / Rust / C++.
- Production experience with streaming pipelines (Kafka, Flink, or similar).
- Experience designing low-latency online stores (Redis, Aerospike, DynamoDB).
- Track record of operating large-scale services with p99 latency SLOs.
- Excellent technical communication and collaboration with research teams.

## Preferred qualifications
- Experience with feature stores (Feast, Tecton) at scale.
- Open-source contributions in ML infrastructure.
- Familiarity with LLM serving (vLLM, TensorRT-LLM) and KV cache management.
- Experience with multi-tenant cost attribution and chargeback.

## Bonus
- Published research or blog posts on ML systems topics.
- Prior staff/principal engineering role at a high-growth company.

Location: San Francisco (hybrid) or remote (US).
